\subsection{Multi-Hop QA and Reasoning Faithfulness}

HotpotQA~\citep{yang2018hotpotqa} and MuSiQue~\citep{trivedi2022musique} remain the canonical multi-hop benchmarks, the latter using controlled single-hop composition to ensure genuine multi-step reasoning is required. Whether models actually reason compositionally is a persistent concern: \citet{min2019compositional} showed that many multi-hop questions can be answered from a single paragraph, \citet{press2023measuring} introduced self-ask prompting to expose intermediate steps, and \citet{turpin2023language} demonstrated that chain-of-thought explanations can be unfaithful to the model's actual computation. The 3-hop final-hop-dominance finding of this work (\S\ref{sec:3hop}) provides quantitative evidence for exactly the shortcut pattern this line of work has flagged.

\subsection{Knowledge Conflicts in LLMs}

\citet{longpre2021entity} first systematically studied the tension between parametric and contextual knowledge, finding that models generally prefer context but that preference varies with conflict type and model confidence. \citet{xie2024adaptive} characterised resolution strategies as ``adaptive chameleons'' versus ``stubborn sloths,'' \citet{chen2022rich} studied multi-source conflicts, and \citet{li2023large} showed that surface-form quality of the conflicting document matters. \citet{pan2023risk} demonstrated that even small amounts of misleading context can significantly degrade factual accuracy. \citet{neeman2023disentqa} and \citet{wang2023resolving} proposed metrics and self-consistency methods for resolution. All of these studies are restricted to single-hop QA, leaving open the question of how conflict position within a reasoning chain affects outcome---the gap this study fills.

\subsection{Retrieval-Augmented Generation}

RAG~\citep{lewis2020retrieval,guu2020realm} has established failure modes relevant to this work: \citet{liu2024lost} documented the ``lost in the middle'' effect, where document position within context matters; \citet{shi2023large} showed that irrelevant context distracts even when correct information is present; \citet{yoran2024making} proposed attribution-based robustness methods. For multi-hop RAG specifically, \citet{khattab2021baleen} and \citet{trivedi2023interleaving} studied iterative retrieval. These works analyse position within a flat retrieval window; this work analyses position across hops in a reasoning chain, which the findings here suggest is a substantively different failure surface.

\subsection{Knowledge Editing and Entity Memorisation}

The strength of parametric memory for specific entities directly shapes conflict resolution. \citet{meng2022locating} (ROME) and \citet{mitchell2022fast} (MEND) demonstrated that factual associations are localisable and editable. \citet{mallen2023when} linked entity popularity to QA accuracy, and \citet{zhu2023physics} showed that frequently-encountered facts are more robustly encoded. These results motivate the entity-popularity analysis in \S\ref{sec:entity_popularity}, which confirms that Wikipedia pageview popularity monotonically predicts parametric override rate under conflict across all four models.
